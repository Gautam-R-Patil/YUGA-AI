Why This Stack?

🧠 Multi-Model AI Strategy

Not a single-API wrapper. We use Groq (LLaMA 3.3 70B) for fast MCQ reasoning, OpenAI GPT-4o for voice-doubt resolution, and GPT-5.4 for structured lecture generation — each model optimized for its task with automatic failover.

🔊 Realistic Avatar + Custom TTS

Lip-syncing 3D video avatar paired with Qwen3-TTS using custom voice instructions ("warm Indian-English tutor") — not generic robot voice. Scientific term pronunciation handled via custom keyword mapping engine.

📊 Custom Algorithms, Not Libraries

Hand-built SM-2 spaced repetition, mastery calculator (weighted: accuracy × recency × difficulty), difficulty adjuster, and recommendation engine. These aren't off-the-shelf — they're purpose-built for exam prep.

⚡ Performance Architecture

Response caching (TTL: 1hr, 100-entry LRU), retry with exponential backoff, compression middleware, 60s timeout with AbortController, and rate limiting — production-ready from day one.

CORE INNOVATION

Voice-First: Learning That Talks Back

The Complete Voice Pipeline

STEP 1 Student Speaks a Doubt

Audio → WebM → Base64 → Google Cloud STT → Text transcription in native language

▼
STEP 2 Translation (if non-English)

Meta NLLB translates Hindi/regional input → English for LLM reasoning

▼
STEP 3 AI Reasoning + Context

GPT-4o processes with conversation history + keyword context + student profile (mastery, score, weak areas)

▼
STEP 4 Voice Synthesis & Avatar Sync

Cleaned speech text → Qwen3-TTS → Natural audio response perfectly synced with the 3D realistic video avatar

What Makes Our Voice Different

🎯 Scientific Keyword Engine

Every question set carries a keyword map (e.g., "K.E." → "Kinetic Energy", "H₂O" → "H two O"). Ambiguous single-letter abbreviations are auto-filtered to prevent incorrect TTS pronunciation. Multi-character abbreviations are expanded for natural speech.

🧩 Context-Aware Responses

YUGA doesn't just answer the question — it knows the student's mastery score, the specific question they're on, the set's keyword vocabulary, and their conversation history. Every response is contextually grounded.

🔒 Scope Enforcement

Unlike ChatGPT, YUGA enforces strict academic scope. Off-topic queries get a polite redirect: "I'm here to help with this specific topic. Let's stay focused on your learning!" — keeping students on track, not distracted.
